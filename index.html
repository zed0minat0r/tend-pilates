Meet your teacher

Jolene Smith

Jolene has practiced Pilates for twenty-five years and has spent more than twenty of them teaching mindful movement. Tend is the studio she built to teach the way she believes Pilates works best: privately, attentively, one person at a time.

Every session starts with how you feel that day. The work is precise and unhurried, built around your body, your history and what you want it to do, never a routine pulled off a shelf.

01What should I wear?

Comfortable, fitted clothing you can move in, so Jolene can see your alignment.

  • Leggings or fitted shorts and a fitted top
  • Grip socks or bare feet
  • Skip loose layers that can catch on the equipment
02What should I expect?

A calm, private session with the full attention on how you move. Your first session starts with a conversation about your goals and any injuries.

  • No experience needed
  • Every session is one to one
  • The equipment is set up for your body each time
03How do I book?

Book online through the schedule in the Book a session section, or call or text 610-316-8242.
